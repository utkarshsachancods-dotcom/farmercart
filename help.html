❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Q: My order is not delivered yet. What to do?

A: Go to My Orders page and check tracking. If it shows Shipped for more than 4 days, call us on 9876543210.

Q: How to pay? Is COD available?

A: Yes, COD (Cash on Delivery), UPI and Card - all 3 are available. You can pay after receiving the product.

Q: Return Policy?

A: 7 Days Easy Return on Damaged Products. Take a photo of the damaged product and WhatsApp us.

Q: Delivery Time?

A: Delivery within 2-4 days in your village / city. Free Delivery above ₹499.

Q: How to become a seller / farmer?

A: Click on Become Seller, fill Name, Mobile Number and Village. Then you can add your own products.

Q: Is fertilizer / seed original?

A: Yes, 100% Original, Company sealed packing. If duplicate, full money will be refunded.

Q: Can I talk to farmer directly?

A: Yes, every product has the farmer's number below it. You can call or WhatsApp directly.

Q: How to track my order?

A: Go to My Orders page - Ordered → Packed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered, every step is visible.
