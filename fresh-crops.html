🥭 How To Identify Fresh Produce?

How To Identify Fresh Produce

Mango: Should have light fragrance, slightly soft when pressed, no spots.
Tomato: Red, shiny, slightly soft when pressed.
Potato: Should be hard, no sprouts.
Onion: Dry, hard, skin should be tight.
Cauliflower: White, tight flower, leaves green.
Spinach: Green, fresh, not wilted.

🏠 How To Store At Home?

1. Keep potato and onion separately.
2. Do not keep tomato in fridge, keep it outside.
3. Wrap spinach and coriander in wet cloth and keep in fridge.
4. Wrap mango in newspaper, it will ripen faster.
