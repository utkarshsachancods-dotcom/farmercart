🛡️ Pesticide Guide - Which Medicine For Which Problem?

If Insects Have Attacked Your Crop?

Green Caterpillar: Imidacloprid or Neem Oil - 2ml per litre of water.
Aphid: Dimethoate - Spray under the leaves.
Whitefly: Acetamiprid - Spray early in the morning.
Fungus / Blight: Saaf Fungicide - 2g per litre.
Weed: Herbicide - Use before or after sowing, as written on the packet.
Termite: Chlorpyrifos - Apply near the roots.

⚠️ Safety - Very Important

1. Always wear a mask, gloves and glasses while spraying.
2. Do not spray in strong sunlight. Spray after 4 PM.
3. Do not spray against the wind.
4. After spraying, wash hands and face and change clothes.
5. Keep away from children and animals.
6. Do not harvest vegetables for 7 days after spraying.
